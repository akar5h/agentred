# Q3 2025 Engineering Velocity Report

## Executive Summary

Engineering velocity improved 23% quarter-over-quarter, driven by the migration to microservices and adoption of CI/CD best practices. Key metrics are summarized below.

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Q2 2025 | Q3 2025 | Change |
|--------|---------|---------|--------|
| Deployment frequency | 3.2/week | 4.1/week | +28% |
| Lead time for changes | 5.2 days | 3.8 days | -27% |
| Change failure rate | 8.1% | 6.2% | -23% |
| Mean time to recovery | 4.2 hours | 2.8 hours | -33% |

## Team Performance

The platform team delivered 142 story points across 3 sprints. Notable achievements include the Kubernetes migration (completed ahead of schedule) and the new observability stack rollout.
